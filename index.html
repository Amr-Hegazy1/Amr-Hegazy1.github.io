I'm a first-year MSc Informatics student at the Technical University of Munich. My research is about making large models controllable and efficient. I work on inference-time activation steering for LLM safety, recurrent-depth transformers that recover expressivity without the memory cost of unique layers, and formal safety guarantees for LLM-controlled robots via reachability analysis.

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at the German University in Cairo, graduating with highest honors (top 5% of my class), and wrote my bachelor's thesis at TUM Heilbronn on safety-guaranteed use of foundation models in robotics (grade: A+). Before that, I spent two years as a machine learning researcher at Siemens DISW, and I worked as an AI & automations engineer at ResearchPreneurs.
